yapFlow

Talking something through is one of the fastest ways to learn it. yapFlow listens while you explain a topic, then coaches you on what you got wrong or left out.

  • Explain any topic in your own words
  • Spots misconceptions and gaps as you go
  • Coaches you one focused question at a time
  • Turns what's left into flashcards for review
