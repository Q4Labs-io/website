$ ./yapflow --demo

Talk it out. Actually learn it.

Our first experiment. You explain a topic in your own words, and yapFlow spots what's missing or wrong, coaches you through it, and turns the gaps into flashcards.

yapflow — topic: computer networking

YOUR YAP

“So, like, TCP is faster than UDP because it, uhh, checks all the packets? And UDP just kind of sends stuff, I guess.”

YAPFLOW

misconception
TCP isn't the faster one

Its checks (handshake, acknowledgements, resends) add overhead. UDP skips them, so it's faster but less reliable.

gap
Why the checks matter

You haven't said what TCP protects against: lost, duplicated or out-of-order packets.
