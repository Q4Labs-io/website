Method

Every idea gets a small build, real users and a number to hit. Hit it, and we double down. Miss it, and we move on.

Build. Focused tools that do one job. LLMs where they help, plain code where they don't.

Test. Real people use it before we polish anything. We watch what they actually do.

Ship. Each experiment has a fixed window and a target set in advance. We keep what hits it.

Yapflow finds

misconception
TCP isn't the faster one

Its checks (handshake, acknowledgements, resends) add overhead. UDP skips them, so it's faster but less reliable.

gap
Why the checks matter

You haven't said what TCP protects against: lost, duplicated or out-of-order packets.
